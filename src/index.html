    FRANCHISE FAQ

    What is the investment range?

    USD 50k–150k per point depending on format: kiosk, food-court or street store.

    What is the payback period?

    3–8 months at target traffic, based on the unit-economics model.

    Do I need food & beverage experience?

    No. Two-week crew training, SOPs and a central kitchen cover operations; experience is a plus.

    Which territories are open?

    Hurghada is live; Cairo, Sharm El Sheikh, Alexandria, Marsa Alam and Luxor are in allocation.

    How does supply work?

    Central kitchen plus approved suppliers with cold-chain logistics.

    Is the product halal?

    Yes, 100% halal-certified suppliers.

    How fast can I open?

    6–10 weeks from signing to opening day.

    What is the royalty?

    Disclosed in the franchise deck after the qualification call.
